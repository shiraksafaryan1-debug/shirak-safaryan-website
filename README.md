# Shirak Safaryan — Academic website

A static, multi-page website. Open `index.html` in a browser or deploy the entire folder to Netlify, GitHub Pages or Vercel.

## Publish a new Analysis & Opinion article
1. Duplicate `article-template.html` and rename it (e.g. `eu-security-analysis.html`).
2. Replace the bracketed title, date and article paragraphs with your own content.
3. Add a linked article card to `analysis.html` under **Latest writing**, for example:

```html
<article class="article-card"><p class="article-meta">26 October 2026 · Analysis</p><h2><a href="eu-security-analysis.html">Your article title</a></h2><p>A short summary.</p><a class="text-link" href="eu-security-analysis.html">Read article ↗</a></article>
```

4. Upload the updated files to your host. The site does not currently include a CMS or login-based editor. For browser-based publishing later, connect a CMS such as Decap CMS, or move the writing section to a CMS platform.
