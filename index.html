RESEARCH · TEACHING · POLICY

Research at the intersection of European politics and regional security.

Based at the University of Wrocław, Shirak Safaryan studies the European Union’s foreign and security policy, EU–Russia relations, and the geopolitical transformations of the South Caucasus. His experience connects academic research, university teaching and public policy.
