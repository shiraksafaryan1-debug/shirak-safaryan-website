Academic biography, institutional experience and educational background.

Academic biography

Shirak Safaryan is a PhD candidate in Political and Administrative Sciences at the University of Wrocław and Academic Research Coordinator at the Yerevan Center of Foreign and Security Policy. His research examines EU foreign and security policy, European integration, EU–Russia relations, and security dynamics in the South Caucasus.

He has held visiting research and fellowship appointments at institutions including the University of Oxford, Maynooth University, Charles University, Ghent University and Sciences Po. His professional background also includes lecturing at the University of Wrocław and policy-facing roles in Armenia.

Safaryan holds master's degrees from the University of Warsaw and Université catholique de Louvain.
